Way of working

How I work with designers, clients and teams.

  1. Stage 1

    Brief

    Design files, content and deadline are shared. With no design yet, I work from a reference site.

  2. Stage 2

    Scope

    Pages, features and delivery date are agreed in writing before work begins.

  3. Stage 3

    Build and review

    Working pages are shared as they are finished, so feedback comes early.

  4. Stage 4

    Launch and handover

    Testing on phones and browsers, publishing, and a walkthrough so the team can update the site.
